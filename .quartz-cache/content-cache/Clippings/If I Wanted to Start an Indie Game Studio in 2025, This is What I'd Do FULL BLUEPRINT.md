#### **1. Introduction**

  - Help aspiring indie developers take the leap.  
  - Personal experience: Started 4 companies, including an indie studio.  
  - Background: Entrepreneurship professor and consultant.

  - Passion for the indie game community.

---

#### **2. Step 1: Forget Your Fear**  
- **Fear is normal and good**  
  - Indicates you care and are stepping out of your comfort zone.  
  - Fear sharpens focus and fuels work.  
- **Embrace the journey**  
  - Struggles and challenges are part of the process.  
  - Satisfaction comes from creating something from nothing.  

---

#### **3. Step 2: Self-Evaluation**  
- **Importance of self-awareness**  
  - Reflect on skills, mindset, and motivations.  
  - Identify gaps and areas for improvement.  
- **Key questions to ask yourself**  
  - Core skills, end goals, organization, financial capacity, time commitment, industry knowledge, and resilience.  
- **Addressing gaps**  
  - Seek partners or funding if needed.  
  - Acknowledge that everyone starts somewhere.  

---

#### **4. Step 3: Know Yourself as a Player**  
- **Balance personal preferences with player needs**  
  - Avoid designing solely for yourself.  
  - Ensure you enjoy your own game.  
- **Play personalities**  
  - Categories of play styles:  
    - **Joker**: Humor and silliness (e.g., *Goat Simulator*).  
    - **Kinesthete**: Movement and rhythm (e.g., *Celeste*).  
    - **Explorer**: Discovery and curiosity (e.g., *Subnautica*).  
    - **Competitor**: Mastery and winning (e.g., *Rocket League*).  
    - Others: Director, Collector, Storyteller, Artist/Creator.  
  - Identify your play style and preferences.  

---

#### **5. Step 4: Get Organized**  
- **Importance of organization**  
  - Prevents projects from stalling.  
- **Tools and techniques**  
  - Tools: Trello, Notion, spreadsheets.  
  - Techniques:  
    - **Pomodoro technique**: Work in short bursts with breaks.  
    - **Time blocking**: Schedule specific tasks during productive hours.  
- **Process example**  
  - Category → Object → Task (e.g., Art → Character Model 3 → Fix arm by Tuesday).  

---

#### **6. Step 5: Determine Time Capacity**  
- **Realistic time management**  
  - Assess weekly availability and productivity.  
  - Be specific about hours and days.  
- **Prioritize tasks**  
  - Focus on the most important aspects of life and game development.  

---

#### **7. Step 6: Set Goals**  
- **Align goals with your mission**  
  - Examples: Build a portfolio, launch a game, learn skills, make friends, earn money.  
- **SMART goals**  
  - **Specific**: Clearly defined.  
  - **Measurable**: Track progress.  
  - **Achievable**: Realistic based on skills and time.  
  - **Relevant**: Align with mission.  
  - **Time-bound**: Set deadlines.  

---

#### **8. Step 7: Ideation Phase**  
- **Brainstorm game ideas**  
  - Consider games you love and market gaps.  
  - Examples of successful innovation:  
    - *Stardew Valley*: Revitalized a genre.  
    - *Minecraft*: Innovated the sandbox genre.  
    - *League of Legends*: Made a genre accessible.  
- **Create a list of ideas**  
  - Use simple, clear descriptions (e.g., *Stardew Valley* in space).  
  - Refine ideas for clarity and feasibility.  

---

#### **9. Step 8: Get Player Feedback**  
- **Importance of validation**  
  - Talk to others about your ideas.  
  - Address concerns about idea theft.  
- **Questions to ask players**  
  - Discovery: What games do you enjoy and why?  
  - Concept feedback: Does this idea interest you? Why or why not?  
  - Specifics: Features, themes, platforms, uniqueness, purchase motivations.  
- **Iterate based on feedback**  
  - Refine ideas and identify gaps in the market.  

---

#### **10. Step 9: Nail Your Player Base**  
- **Formula for success**  
  - Other players' fun + Your fun + Unique flavor = Potential success.  
- **Persona document**  
  - Define your target audience:  
    - Demographics, gaming preferences, motivations, challenges, play style, habits.  
  - Use data from player feedback and market research.  
- **Avoid analysis paralysis**  
  - Gather enough data to make informed decisions, but don't overanalyze.  

---

#### **11. Final Step: Overcome Fear Again**  
- **Take the leap**  
  - All preparation leads to this moment.  
  - Embrace the unknown and start your journey.  
- **Next steps**  
  - Address legal, financial, and logistical aspects as you go.  
  - Use resources like the free PDF blueprint for guidance.
