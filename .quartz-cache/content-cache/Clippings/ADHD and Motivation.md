### ADHD and Motivation: How to Build Your "Motivation Bridge"

####   [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=11) The Problem: Intention vs. Action
*   [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=15) There's a "chasm" between intending to do something and actually doing it.
*   [00:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=19) For most people, this chasm is bridged by "[[Motivation Bridge]]."
	*   [00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=25) Built with [[motivation|motivational]] "planks" (e.g., career benefits, pleasing family, keeping promises).
	*   [00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=34) Small gaps can be overcome with willpower and self-discipline.

####   [00:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=45) ADHD and the "Motivation Deficit"
*   [00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=49) ADHD is often characterized by a "motivation deficit disorder" (Dr. Russell Barkley).
*   [00:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=51) Importance doesn't translate into as many motivational planks as it does for neurotypical individuals.
*   [00:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=59) Large gaps in motivation = "Half the bridge is missing."
*   [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=102) Leads to frustration because the desire and effort are present, but the action isn't.
*   [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=110) Misunderstandings from others who assume a lack of care or [[laziness]].
*   [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=125) Internal frustration because past successes create confusion about current struggles.
*   [01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=139) Success depends on having enough "planks" in place.

####   [01:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=149) Fixing Motivation Bridge: Understanding ADHD Motivation
*   [01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=150) Key: Understand what *does* and *doesn't* motivate ADHD brains.
*   [01:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=158) Challenges: Lengthy, repetitive, or boring tasks.
*   [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=204) Motivators:
	*   [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=208) [[urgency]]
	*   [02:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=209) [[novelty]]
	*   [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=211) Personal Interest
*   [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=213) Essentially, things that are stimulating and engaging.
*   [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=217) Relates to why stimulant medication and exercise are helpful.
*   [02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=221) This need for stimulation is not a choice, it is how the brain functions.
*   [02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=225) Some tasks naturally possess these motivators (e.g., video games).
*   [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=230) For others, we need to *add* them.

####   [02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=234) How to Fill in the Planks:
#####  [02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=234) Make it Urgent
*   [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=236) [[procrastination]] creates unintentional urgency.
*   [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=249) Not the healthiest method.
*   [02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=251) Alternative Strategies:
	*   [02:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=253) Artificial deadlines (hit-and-miss, since they aren't perceived as "real").
	*   [02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=259) [[Accountability]] (making deadlines "real").
		*   [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=302) Examples: Draft deadlines with teachers, progress check-ins with coworkers, inviting someone over to force cleaning.
	*   [03:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=319) Realize inherent urgency: connect current tasks to future consequences.
	*   [03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=331) Timers: Compete against the clock.
*   [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=336) What *doesn't* help: Vague, open-ended deadlines ("get it to me whenever").
	*   [03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=348) Instead, provide check-ins and shame-free accountability.
#####  [03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=353) Make it New/Novel
*   [03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=355) New environments, new methods.
	*   [03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=355) Examples: Homework in a hammock, new laundry folding techniques (Marie Kondo), varied floss flavors.
*   [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=412) Accept hobby cycling: put things down and revisit them when they feel fresh.
*   [04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=421) Iterate on routine tasks: find easier, better, faster, or weirder ways to do them.
*   [04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=431) Gamification.
#####  [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=436) Make it Personally Interesting
*   [04:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=438) ADHD brains are interest-based learners.
*   [04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=441) Interest determines ability to complete tasks.
*   [05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=500) Actively seek interest *before* starting a task.
	*   [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=505) Example: This channel focuses on topics that are personally relevant to the creator's current struggles.
*   [05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=512) Even with required tasks, find a personally interesting angle or approach.
*   [05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=523) Avoid pitting necessary tasks against more interesting ones.
*   [05:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=528) Add interesting rewards.
	*   [05:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=533) Rewards are highly motivating for ADHD brains.
	*   [05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=536) **Prefer short-term rewards**: Time perception differences mean long-term rewards are less effective.
	*   [05:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=543) Immediate feedback (sticker charts, raffle tickets, verbal acknowledgement) is best.
	*   [05:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=549) Shortens the time between behavior and reward.

####   [06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=603) Conclusion:
*   [06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=603) These strategies (urgency, novelty, interest) add "planks" to the Motivation Bridge.
*   [06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=603) Not all planks need to be in place at once.
*   [06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=610) Don't waste energy on sheer willpower when large gaps exist.
*   [06:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OM0Xv0eVGtY&t=619) Instead, identify missing planks and fill them in.
