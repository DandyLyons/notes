## Description
In an age of unlimited information, proactively choosing what we fill our eyes, ears, and hearts with is more important than ever. The articles we read, the podcasts we listen to, and the videos we consume have a profound effect on our beliefs and how we see the world. Are you being wise in what you consume? In this episode, I talk about the reasons, principles, and tools I use to curate my own information diet and offer some practical tips for you as well.
