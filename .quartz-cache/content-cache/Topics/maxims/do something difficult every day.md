> If you don't feel like doing it, then **do it**. Those kinds of things train your mind in the right kind of way to take on the things in your life that you know you should be doing but keep avoiding because they're hard. 

## Benefits
This practice encourages [[resilience]], [[responsibility]], and [[habits]].
