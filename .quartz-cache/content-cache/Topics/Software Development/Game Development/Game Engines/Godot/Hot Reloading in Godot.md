> [!NOTE] Hot Reloading
> *The `reloadable` flag enables automatic reloading of your extension by the editor every time you recompile it, without needing to restart the editor. This only works if you compile your extension in debug mode (default).*
> - From [Godot Docs](https://arc.net/l/quote/dknanzrp)
