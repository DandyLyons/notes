[Steam Broadcasting](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/548F-BC55-89EB-1BC8)  allows you to watch your friends play their favorite games. You can also set up Broadcasting to allow others to watch you play.  
  
Read more about this feature from the [official update](https://steamcommunity.com/updates/broadcasting?snr=100601___).
