Common Works Registration (CWR) is a data format that is overseen by [CISAC](https://www.cisac.org/), and is the globally recognized standard for the registration and revision of [musical works](https://help.themlc.com/en/support/what-is-a-musical-work). Its file format enables the efficient tracking of registration status and allows for better communication between [music publishers](https://help.themlc.com/en/support/what-is-a-music-publisher), Mechanical Rights Societies, and [Performing Rights Organizations (PROs)](https://help.themlc.com/en/support/what-is-a-performing-rights-organization-pro).
