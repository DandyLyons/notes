

## My Difficulties with this doctrine
- [[2 Pet-03#v9|2 Peter 3:9]], [[1 Tim-02#v4|1 Timothy 2:4-7]], 
- [[John-01|John 1]]: 
	- [[John 1.1]]: [[the Word]] is [[Elohim|God]]. 
	- [[John 1.4]]: In Him, (who is The Word and who is God) is life. and the life was the **light of men**
	- [[John 1.5]]: Light (truth and righteousness) overcomes darkness (lies and sin). This is the power of God, to overpower our sinful nature, by revealing the truth to us and in so doing give us life. 
	- [[John 1.9]]: The light gives light (life) to everyone. 
	- So we see: God is the Word, is life, is the **light** of men, and the **light** gives life to **everyone**.
