- **"You Will Lose This Game": A Game About Failure**
	- A game about adulting. 
	- Inspirations: 
		- BitLife
	- Gameplay: 
		- Decisions are made by clicking options. However each options has a timer, and when the timer runs out, the option will automatically be picked. Hidden game mechanics (such as tiredness, anger, sadness etc.) cause timers to move faster or slower. If the player engages in self-reflection, then some of these game mechanics will begin to be shown to the player. 
	- Emergent story telling
		- The story is non-linear with multiple endings. But it does not follow the typical branching path story structure. 
		- Instead, gameplay decisions mutate variables which trigger story events. Characters in later story events will reference prior story events. Each story events can only trigger if the necessary conditions are met. 
		- Instead of needing to account for every possible branching path, we will simply create many story events. Each play session will have a unique combination of story events which will generate a unique story. Thus replays will allow players to experience new story events and content. 
		- Many story events will be mutually exclusive such that if event A happens, then event B is no longer possible. 
- Cops & Robbers
	- 5-10 minute matches
	- Each match starts with the robbers stealing from a bank and then driving their getaway
	- The cops have to catch the robbers and the robbers have to get away. 
	- If the robbers win then their "high score" is their bounty. If the cops win then their "high score" is the court sentencing for the robbers.
