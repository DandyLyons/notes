- Scripture Reading: [[Luke-08|Luke 8]] , [[Matt-08|Matthew 8]] 
- Key Verse: [[Matt-08#v22|Matt 8.22]] But Jesus said to him, "Follow me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead."  
- Verse Reflection: I feel conflict because of this verse. To follow Jesus I must leave this world behind, at least in some sense. 

### Prayer
- Gratitude: I'm thankful that my wife appreciates me. 
- Specific Prayers: Lord, I pray for protection from temptation for all the men in my CR group. Today, let it be known that their Father sees them and hears them, and the Son is their Brother, and the Spirit is their Helper. 
- Praise: Lord I praise You for the marvelous works you do in this world, stirring the stubborn hearts of men. 

### Personal Application
- How can this scripture guide me today?
- Challenges I'm facing: I struggle to follow Jesus, much less leave behind this world. But this world I leave behind is dead. Why should I follow the dead? 
- Commitments to God: Lord, I commit to follow you now. 

### End of Day Review
