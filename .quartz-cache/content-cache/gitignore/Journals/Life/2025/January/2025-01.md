## Log
### Goal: Establish systems for building healthy habits this quarter
> [!attention] Don't Let **"[[Tool Fiddling]]"** Become a [[Topics/distractions/index|Distraction]]
> This month I've focused on establishing systems, and at a practical level that has required me setting up a bunch of tools. 👇🏼 Tools are invaluable because they can help you to do more with less. But tools can also become a distraction! We often like to fiddle with our tools[^1] rather than actually use them! This is a difficult balance. An unoptimized tool is slow, frustrating, inefficient, and sometimes even dangerous[^2]. But why even have a tool if you spend all your time fiddling instead of using it. 

- [d] Failure: I've worked out once this month. **I haven't established a workout habit.** I need to hire a trainer. 
- [w] Onboarded myself for using [[Tasks plugin]]
	- Before, I found it vital to create [[gitignore/Journals/Life/TEMPLATES/Daily Notes TEMPLATE|Daily Notes]]. These help me to: 
		- process my thoughts
		- save my learnings
	- But the **problem** that I had was that tasks often span across multiple days. This creates friction as I have tasks strewn across my vault, and I waste time and focus trying to find relevant tasks. 
	- The Tasks plugin provides [[Tasks plugin#Querying Tasks|queries]]. Much like Dataview queries, these make it much easier to resurface tasks in new contexts. The syntax is simpler than Dataview. But more importantly it is aware of relative dates, so it seems easier to add to periodic notes such as weekly or monthly notes. 
- [w] Onboarded myself for [[Projects Plugin]]
	- Interestingly, projects are not my favorite use case for this plugin. What I love this plugin for is AirTable-like databases. This plugin allows me to edit properties of multiple files and view them as tables, calendars, etc. 
- [w] Onboarded myself for [[Journals Plugin]]
	- This has been a fantastic replacement for the [[Periodic Notes]] plugin and calendar notes plugin. 
	- [?] Right now I have two journals: **Life**[^3] and **Devotions**. I wonder if it's better to just remove the devotions journal and add it to my life journal. 
- Try [[Meta Bind]] plugin. 
	- I'm still experimenting with this plugin but it has lessened my reliance on Dataview and it does have potential. Definitely a risk for [[tool fiddling]].
