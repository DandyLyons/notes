## Hiring Process
- Selected for Interview
- Screening Interview(s) (1 or 2 rounds)
	- Typically a **remote** interview
	- Often a **technical** interview
- In-Person Interviews (3-6 rounds)
	- The "Lunch" Interview: 
		- one of these rounds is typically done during lunch. 
		- usually not technical
		- This is a good chance to ask culture questions
