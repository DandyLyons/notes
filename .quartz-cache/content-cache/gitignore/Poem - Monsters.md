There are monsters, yes monsters, in caverns so deep 
Ghouls in the shadows and bats in a heap. 
Slimy ones, scaly ones, bloody impaling ones. 
These are the monsters that steal all my sleep. 

There’s a monster who lives inside of my head. 
Who selfishly sleeps on my side of the bed. 
Only I can hear the awful things that he’s said
Or felt his odium when his blood boils red. 

The pain of hearing my lover cry herself to sleep
The searing pain of knowing that I'm the one who causes her to weep
These are the monsters that steal all my sleep
