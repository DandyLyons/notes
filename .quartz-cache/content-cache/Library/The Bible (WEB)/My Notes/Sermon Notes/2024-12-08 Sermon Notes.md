## Points

- the **3 marks of the true church**?
	- Christian discipline
- **2 keys to Christian discipline** 
	- Pastoral censure
		- This is what people usually think of when they think of church discipline
	- Faithfully keeping God's word
		- We must not forget that this is a necessary and good part of discipline in the church. 
- the **proper way to exercise Christian discipline**
	- [["two or three witnesses"]]: 
		- Principle: We should not simply stoop to *he said, she said*, hearsay, misinformation. Instead, when one makes an accusation against another, there ought to be multiple witnesses corroborating the accusation. This way we mitigate against man's sinful ability to make up false accusations. 
- God is a God of **order**: 
	- in employment: 
		- He tells employers how they ought to treat workers, and how workers ought to treat employers and fellow employees
	- in the family: 
		- How parents ought to treat their children and vice versa
	- in the Government: 
		- How rulers ought to treat citizens and vice versa
		- The ruler's role is to punish evildoers. 
		- **The goal** is to keep alive the knowledge of God and fulfill the moral law of God. 
	- in the Church: 
		- God establishes offices: ministers, apostles, pastors, elders etc. 
		- God delegates some of his authority to ministers in the Church
		- **The role of leaders in the church** is to be medicine and reform the believer. 
		- **The goal** is to maintain the purity and sanctification of the whole church. 
			- This is a more direct way than the civil govt. 
			- We rejoice and weep with our fellow believers in the church. It is a family of God's people. 
		- Discipline in the Church Must follow the order established by God in scripture: 
			- [[Matt-18#If Your Brother Sins Against You...|Matthew 18:15-20]]
			- **We should not bring our grievances directly to the church first.** Instead we ought to confront our brother directly first in love ([[Gal-06#v1|Galatians 6:1]]), faithfulness, and compassion. Under the authority of Christ and His Word. [[Matt-18#v15|Matthew 18:15]] 
				- This should be done face to face. Not in a letter, or public gossip. 
				- We should be humble, Godly and show concern for our brother, as a friend, seeking their best welfare. [[Prov-27#v6|Proverbs 27:6]] 
				- We should lovingly state "*I'm not better or different from you.*"
				- **Case study:** [[Isa-22#v19|Isaiah 22:19]]: God charged Shebna because he was showing corrupt partiality. So God took Shebna's power and gave it to David's house. 
- [[keys in the Bible]]: 
	- 2 big keys in the Church that open and shut the gates of Heaven
		- the key of preaching
			- This is primarily positive. It is discipline that instructs and exhorts. 
			- Preaching is God's [[keys in the Bible|key]] of authority, which opens and shuts the gates of Heaven. 
				- It is God's "[[thus says the LORD]]" 
			- It is also exclusionary, discriminatory: 
				- It separates the sheep from the goats. 
				- It corrects and reproves us. It separates sinners and hypocrites from the church. 
			- [[Matt-18#v18|Matthew 18:18]]: *"whatever things you bind on earth will have been bound in heaven, and whatever things you release on earth will have been released in heaven."*
				- **release on earth**: the preacher proclaims that the sinner will be saved if they repent. 
				- **bind on earth:** the preacher proclaims that the sinner will remain damned if they refuse to repent. 
			- Preaching should allow a person to understand if they are or are not a true believer. 
		- the key of exercising of [[Church discipline]] : 
			- ⭐: ❓ If [[preaching]] does all these things, why then do we still need [[Church discipline]]? **A**: Because we live in a sinful, fallen world. 
				- But God wants to keep His Church pure. 
					- We are called not be conformed to this world. To be separated, holy. We ought to break from every known sin in our lives. 
					- ⭐ If you are struggling with addiction to sin, go to your minister and get help to turn to Christ. 
					- You cannot live with sin and prosper spiritually. You are destroying yourself. 
				- **The Honor of God is at stake:**
					- If the Church is hypocritical, it reflects poorly on God
				- Two problems that ought to be solved with Church discipline: 
					- 1. **false heretical doctrine**:
					- 2. **persistent unrepentant sinful lifestyle**: 
				- Church discipline is a **blessing**: 
					- 1. It aims for the restoration of the sinner. Healing
					- 3. It brings glory to God. 
- **hypocrites in the church**: appear to be righteous but it is just an act, a mask
	- They do not: 
		- grieve of their sins, admit their wrongs
		- repent and change their ways
## Application
- ⭐ If you are struggling with addiction to sin, go to your minister and get help to turn to Christ.
