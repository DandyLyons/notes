## 📖Bible Passage
>[!NOTE]+ [[Ps-105#v3|Ps 105:3-4]] 
>Glory in his holy name. Let the heart of those who seek Yahweh rejoice.  Seek Yahweh and his strength. Seek his face forever more.  [[Ps-105#v4|]]


## Points

- We are commanded in this verse to seek the LORD. 
	- Calvin teaches that to seek God is the most important thing a Christian can and must do. 
### God calls us to seek Him
- ["] "**Let the heart of those who rejoice, seek the LORD.**"
	- The first thing we are commanded in our text today is to seek the LORD. 
- God calls us to seek Him throughout the Bible.

- Here God gives us permission to rejoice, and he **promises** that we **shall** rejoice. 
- ⭐ All people are seekers. 
	- Even the ungodly seek. 
		- Our hearts are large but they are empty, and they are always seeking something more. We are always reaching out. Exploring. Discovering. Searching. 
			- This is the way that God made us. 
			- We are vessels that need to be filled. 
			- But the problem is that sin has blinded us, meaning that now, we do not seek the LORD. 
				- We seek vain pleasures and even wicked sinful things. 
				- We ought to seek God who is an ocean of good an grace. 
- Jesus is our guiding light and we need to seek Him. 
	- But in this world there are so many other distracting lights. Imposter lights. These lights lead to death whereas Jesus is the light that leads to life. 
	- So Jesus calls the Church to be the light on the hill, shining to the world, so that the world may see Christ and be saved. 
		- >Let your light shine. 
- Notice that God commands us three times to seek Him. 
	- Why does God have to repeat Himself? Because we are so easily distracted and we need this reminder. 
	- Three, also reminds us that God is triune and we must seek all three persons of the trinity. 
	- God is one God, with One Name, but He reveals Himself in three persons.

### Seek His Face Continually
- Our text calls us to seek Him three times but it uses His name only two times. On this last time, we are called to seek His face. 
- Why are we called to seek His face? 
	- One reason, is to show us that God is not hiding from us. He is not hiding His face from us. 
		- He **wants** us to seek Him. He wants to be sought. 
		- The problem is not with God's face, but ours. 
		- The problem is not that God's back is toward us. The problem is that **our back** is toward Him. 
		- ⭐ What did Adam and Eve do after they sinned? They *hid themselves from the face of God*. [[Gen-03#v8|Gen 3.8]] 
			- But despite the fact that they hid from Him, God was seeking them. 
			- And God is still seeking you and I. 
		- [[Num-06#v25|Num 6:25-26]] Yahweh make his **face** to shine on you, and be gracious to you.  Yahweh lift up his face toward you, and give you peace.'  [[Num-06#v26|]] 
	- Another reason why He uses the word *face* is to assure us that He wants to have **fellowship and communion and intimacy** with Him. 
		- It is not as though He only wants obedient servants with no relationship. No, He wants to be close to us. 
	- A third reason, is because the Holy Spirit is teaching us about Jesus Christ. A face is a human thing. God, Himself is invisible, but Jesus has a face. 
		- Jesus is the image of the invisible God. [[Col-01#v15|Col 1.15]] 
## Application
-  We must turn to God, **through Christ**. Seek Him, through Jesus Christ. 
	- Seek Him through His face, who is Christ. 
	- We are desperately separated from Him because of our sin, but in Christ we can seek and find Him. 
	- We must **only seek God in Christ**. 
		- We can only know God through His son. 
		- Christ is the meeting place where we meet with God. 
		- Outside of Christ, God is a consuming fire. 
- It is a delightful thing to seek the LORD. We can and we must rejoice in Him. 
	- In Song of Solomon, the Bride seeks *Him whom my heart love*. And this is what we must do toward God. 
	- [[Jer-29#v13|Jer 29.13]] **You shall seek me, and find me**, when you search for me with all your heart.  
- God is our strength
	- Seek the LORD and His strength. 
	- We find our strength in Him. 
	- we cannot find our strength in ourselves or in anything else. 
	- Jesus did not die in cowardice. In His strength, He faced death head on. Oh how great is His strength! 
- See His face evermore. 
	- Seeking God is not something you only do on the day you are converted. 
	- It is something we do continually every day. 
	- True Christianity in its essence is **continually** seeking the LORD. 
		- It is persevering without ever stopping. 
			- To stop is to be not genuine. 
	- Guard yourself from idolatry for it causes us to stop seeking Him, and to seek vain idols.
