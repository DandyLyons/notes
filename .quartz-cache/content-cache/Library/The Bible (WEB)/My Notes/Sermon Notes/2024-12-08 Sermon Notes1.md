## 📖Bible Passage
>[!NOTE]+ [[Ps-104#v33|Psalm 104:33]]
>I will sing to Yahweh as long as I live. I will sing praise to my God while I have any being.  
## Points

- Christ sings praise to God, in and amongst His people. 
	- [[Heb-13#v12|Heb 13.12]]
- Here in our text, **the LORD teaches us how we should view life**. ==We should **always** sing praise to God==. 
	- Psalm 104:33: *I will sing to Yahweh <u>as long as I live</u>. I will sing praise to my God **<u>while I have any being</u>.* 
	- [[Phil-04#v4|Philippians 4:4-7]][[Phil-04#v5|]][[Phil-04#v6|]][[Phil-04#v7|]]: *Rejoice in the LORD always.* 
		- Then you will find perfect peace. 
	- [[1 Thess-05#v16|1 Thess. 5:16-18]] ==Always rejoice.  Pray without ceasing.==  In everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus toward you.  [[1 Thess-05#v17|]][[1 Thess-05#v18|]] 
	- **No matter what happens, always praise God.** 
		- It is a powerful and empowering way to live your life. 
			- It's empowering because you cannot know what will happen in your life, but you can always know how to respond, with praise and thanksgiving to Him. 
	- We praise Him because He commands us. 
	- It is also an opportunity for us. 
- This psalm highlights the innumerable wonderful things of God which are worthy of praise. 
	- We struggle to feed a multitude of people, and yet God feeds animals all across creation effortlessly. 
- Whatever you are doing, wherever you go, **your heart needs to continually praise God** for who He is. 
	- The more we praise Him, the more we will find ourselves eagerly coming to Him with praise at church on the LORD's day. 
		- We should be praising Him throughout the week, wherever we go. 
		- ❓: Who has more reason to praise Him for salvation, grace, and sanctification than Christians? 
			- **Christians ought to praise Him more than any other people.** 
- There are so many scriptural examples of praises toward God:
	- [[Ps-63#v3|Ps 63:3-5]] Because your loving kindness is better than life, my lips shall praise you.  So I will bless you while I live. I will lift up my hands in your name.  My soul shall be satisfied as with the richest food. My mouth shall praise you with joyful lips,  [[Ps-63#v4|]][[Ps-63#v5|]] 
		- This is a resolution to continually praise Him
	- [[Ps-100|Psalm 100]]
	- [[Ps-135|Psalm 135]] 
	- [[Ps-145|Psalm 145]]
