## 📖Bible Passage
>[!NOTE]+ 2 Corinthians 12:20
>For I am afraid that by any means, when I come, I might find you not the way I want to, and that I might be found by you as you don't desire, that by any means there would be strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, factions, slander, whisperings, proud thoughts, or riots, 

## Points

- **How did the believers in Corinth have their minds corrupted?**
	- False apostles and teachers had come into this congregation in Corinth. They preached another gospel and another spirit. 
		- [[Ephes-04#v11|Ephesians 4:11-16]] 
		- The building up of the Church, or edifying, cannot happen without the ministry of Christ. 
- **What is the purpose of the ministry of the Gospel?** 
	- The work of the ministry is to promote your spiritual growth. 
	- **It is not the people, nor the minister who determines what the minister should do. <u>It is Christ Himself.</u>** 
		- It is Christ who decides who is apostle, prophet evangelist etc. (Ephes. 4:11)
	- [[Matt-16#v18|Matthew 16:18]]
- **What is the only way to build up, edify, the Body of Christ?** 
	- to preach the Word of God. 
		- The Word of God is the **only** word which builds up the body of Christ? 
		- The Word of God was not being preached in Corinth at this time. 
- **What does Paul fear?**
	- Paul feared: 
		- That the people of Corinth would have fallen back into sinful living. 
		- That the people of Corinth would also find Paul sorrowing in them. 
			- Disappointed because he love them. 
				- If he didn't love them, he wouldn't sorrow over them. 
- **What sins does the LORD list in our text?**
	- These sins are not the work of the Holy Spirit. 
		- All these sins destroy unity in the church, among families and relationships among people. 
	- 4 of these sins are also listed in [[Gal-05#v20|Galatians 5:20]] 
	- The many lists of sins in the bible are not meant to be exhaustive lists. 
	- These 8 sins are all rooted in self-love: 
		- 1. contentions (strife)
			- Debates, quarreling. 
			- We see this when the Corinthians say *We are of Paul, and we are of Apollos*. 
		- 2. jealousies
			- self-centered narrowness, that is suspicious of anyone outside of one's faction. 
			- they wish to curse others and not bless them. 
			- Cain was jealous of Abel. The pharisees were jealous of Jesus. The elder brother was jealous of the prodigal son. 
			- Jealousy is born of hell. The devil himself was jealous of God
		- 3. outbursts of wrath (anger): 
			- unreasonable fits of rage
		- 4. selfish ambitions (factions)
			- divisiveness, party division
		- 5. backbitings, (slander)
			- The unnecessary bickering of opposers. Loudmouth criticisms and insults
		- 6. Whisperings, 
			- Quiet whispers of criticisms. Gossip. 
		- 7. Conceits (proud thoughts): 
			- manifestations of pride, arrogance, puffing up, thinking of yourself more highly than you ought to think. 
		- 8. tumults (riots): 
			- anarchy, everyone doing what is right in their own eyes. 
- Paul warns us what will happen if we follow these sinful actions 
	- [[Gal-05#v15|Galatians 5:15]]: "*But if you bite and devour one another, be careful that you don't consume one another.*"
		- If we continue all this sinful strife against each other **we will consume (destroy) each other.** 
	- The bible says, those who do such things **will not inherit the Kingdom of Heaven**. 
		- Doing good works also will not gain you access to the Kingdom of God. 
			- Even our good works are as filthy rags. [[Isa-64#v6|Isaiah 64:6]]
		- Only faith in Christ may bring you into the Kingdom of God. 
			- The way to be saved is not to cease from bad works, nor is it to do good works. **It is too late for that.** 
			- The way to be saved is to repent of your sins and to believe if the Lord Jesus Christ. This is the Gospel. 
			- When we see this list of sins, it is very important that we do not send the message "You must not do these things. You must become Christians." This is a very confusing message to non-believers indeed. It makes them think that they could possibly earn their own salvation. 
		- Sinful men, women, and. children will not enter the Kingdom of God **as they are**. 
		- ⭐: We should keep away from these sins because they will bar us from Heaven. 
- Look at the list of the [[Fruit of the Spirit]]: 
	- We see it in [[Gal-05#v22|Galatians 5:22]]: 
		- Love: 
			- This is not a sentimental trite love. 
		- Joy
		- Peace: 
			- It's not a fragile peace. It is the peace of God. 
		- Longsuffering
		- Kindness
		- Goodness
		- Faithfulness
		- Gentleness
		- Self-control 
	- The Fruit of the Spirit is the **opposite** of the works of the flesh. 
		- Harmony instead of strife. 
		- soberness instead of drunkenness. 
	- Let these words sink into your heart and soul. Let them be your way of life. 
		- This is the only way to enter into Heaven. 
		- These are indeed the communicable attributes of God. 
		- It is the goodness of God at work in our lives. 
		- It is the Lord God Himself, Christ Himself, the Holy Spirit Himself living inside of you. 
		- What a wonderful way to live. 
		- May God grant that we keep well away from the flesh, the world, and the devil. 
			- The trouble is that we flirt with sins. 
			- These sins are poisons and monsters.
