## 📖Bible Passage
>[!NOTE]+ Psalm 104:24
>Yahweh, how many are your works! In wisdom, you have made them all. The earth is full of your riches. 

## Points

- In the summer we are able to spend more time outside and thus we are brought face to face with God, His glory and and the creation He made. 
	- Our mouths and all our senses fail us in doing justice to God's great creation. 
	- Our minds are inadequate to comprehend the extent of God's creation. 
- What do we experience every time we go outside? 
	- We see the handiwork of God. This should cause us to be in awe and wonder of Him. 
- How could we curse the weather? The rain, snow, winds etc. These are the works of God. They are His servants sent to fulfill His purposes. 
	- [[Ps-148#v7|Psalm 148:7-12]]: There is nothing in creation that works or acts without God. 
	- **Natural law**: These are the normal laws of how nature usually works. Everything in creation has order. 
		- And yet God can suspend this natural law at any time. 
		- He did so on numerous occasions in miracles. 
- There is a wrong view which says that God is in nature. That God is in the earth.
	- This is not the case. God is above nature, outside of it. 
- communism tries to take the place of God. 
	- communism places itself as the rightful authority to decide who gets what. 
- [[Job-26#v14|Job 26:14]]: We only see such a small glimpse of who God is. but the fullness of His power is beyond our imagination and grasp. 
	- Psalm 104:24: the works of God are so many
- [[Ps-19#v1|Psalm 19:1]]: The heavens declare the glory of God and the firmament shows his handiwork. 
- ⭐: God created this universe, not primarily for us to exploit it, but to increase our faith and to spur us to rely on Him. 
- Do not harden your heart. Come to Him. Taste, see and enjoy all that God has given to us. 
	- When you do you will be strengthened. 
	- And you will enjoy God who has given all these things to us.
