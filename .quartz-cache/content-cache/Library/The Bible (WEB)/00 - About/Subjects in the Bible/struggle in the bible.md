If God is [[God is good|God is all-good]] and [[God is powerful|God is all-powerful]], then why do we have struggle in this life? This is certainly an important question worth asking. Humanity has been asking this question for thousands of years. It's also a very difficult question to answer and has become [[theodicy|an entire area of study in theology]], which we won't fully cover right here. 

However I would like to highlight something: **struggle is found throughout the Bible**. The authors of the Bible do not hide the paradox of struggle. In fact, struggle is not only throughout the Bible, it is actually a core part of the Bible. In fact, when God chose His people, He named them... struggle? 

## Israel Means Struggles with God
God chose His people to be the descendants of [[Jacob]], and then He gave Jacob a new name: *Israel*. We read this account in [[Gen-32|Genesis 32]].
