Sold 12.5k after about 3 weeks. 
12.5k X $10 = $125,000 gross revenue
(Don't forget some copies might have been sold on sale.)
### [06:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ycSvC0ZM0k&t=401s) - Money
[10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=617&v=5ycSvC0ZM0k): After fees, expenses, taxes, Steam's cut etc. there's about 43% left over. 
~ $125,000 x 43% = ~ $53,750 profit
This is estimated profits from 3 weeks of sales. Don't forget the game took about 3 years for him to make.
