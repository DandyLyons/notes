My Notes

Steam

Keep 100% revenue with Steam Keys

  • 30:36: Steam regularly takes 30%, but if you sell steam keys, they take 0%.
    • 31:37: You can sell your steam keys on other platforms such as itch.io, humble bundle etc.

Getting Views on Steam

  • 32:09: Visibility Rounds:
    • Steam gives you 5 VRs
    • When you use a VR, you get 500k impressions on the front page of Steam.
    • When you leave early access you get 1M impressions.
    • Join Steam sales for more impressions.

Piracy on Steam

  • 33:41
    • Steam doesn’t have protections for piracy
    • The real problem of piracy is access and cost. If people are stealing your game, it’s because it is difficult to access, or it is prohibitively expensive.
    • Recommendation: Make sure that you localize your prices. Set your prices in each country to a price that is not prohibitively expensive for that region.
