	- Quotes (can import from API)
- Focus Filters
- Import/Export
	- JSON and Markdown, CSV
- Onboarding with [[Topics/Software Development/Apple Platforms/index|TipKit]] 
- Longterm vision/goals
- **Add to my toolbox** button
	- The app will download JSON content updates of new *tools*. By default, these tools will appear before users[^1]. If the user likes, they can tap **Add to my toolbox** to copy the tool into their toolbox.
