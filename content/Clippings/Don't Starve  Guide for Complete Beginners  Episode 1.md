## Description
Don't Starve is an amazing survival game that has now become iconic. It is a brutal game, and it intentionally leaves much of the game unexplained, to be figured out through trial and error--and usually death. Don't Starve is can be overwhelming and this guide aims to help you get a foothold. In this guide for complete beginners to Don't Starve, I will start a brand new game and walk you through the basics of the controls, the UI, and tips and tricks to help you survive in the beginning of the game and learn how to grow and evolve crafting abilities, tools, and survival chances on your own terms. This is a completely non-spoiler guide, meant only to help you learn the fundamentals so you can explore and enjoy the surprises Don't Starve offers on your own. This guide is meant for you to play along or watch to hear my thought process and see how everything is done within the flow and context of an actual game.

Come learn to play Don't Starve!
