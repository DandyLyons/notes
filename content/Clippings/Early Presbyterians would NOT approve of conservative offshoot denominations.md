### What about Machen? 
- [20:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1209&v=wHIhoLZaBfI): You may say Machen was kicked out of the PCUSA? No he was not. 
	- Machen was defrocked (removed from ministry), not excommunicated. 
	- Even if it were legitimate for Machen to split from the PCUSA, Machen could have united with the many existing convervative presbyterian denominations, but instead he created his own. 
	- [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1265&v=wHIhoLZaBfI): "*Machen was a schismatic. Machen was not to be respected.*" 

### Must all Protestants unite? 
- [21:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1285&v=wHIhoLZaBfI): 
	- ❓: What is the difference between *non-union* and *separation*? 
		- Redeemed Zoomer makes a point that currently conservative protestant churches have communion with each other, despite being separate denominations. 
- [22:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1365&v=wHIhoLZaBfI): Difference between Rutherford's view and Redeemed Zoomer
	- Rutherford argues that Christians should leave separatists and reunite with the mainline churches. 
	- Redeemed Zoomer recognizes that we need Christians to stay in the *separatist* churches so that they can push for their churches to reunite with the mainline churches. 

### The Long-Term Value of Institutional Churches

	- [31:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1872&v=wHIhoLZaBfI): "*Once schism becomes an option, it's gonna happen eventually.*"
