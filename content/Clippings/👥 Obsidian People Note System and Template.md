## Description
This video is a tutorial on my Obsidian People Note system. Follow along to set up your own personal CRM.

In this system, there's a centralized People MOC (Map of Contents) page that lists all your People Notes, and includes a button to create a new People Note from a template. We'll also be creating that template to use in your system.

This system pairs nicely with my Meeting Note Template system. On each People Note, you'll see a list of every meeting that person has been in. All you need to do is link to that person in the Meeting note.
