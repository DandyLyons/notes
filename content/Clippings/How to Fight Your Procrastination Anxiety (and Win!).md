### Introduction
*   [00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=17) ADHD and Chronic [[procrastination]]: People with ADHD tend to be chronic procrastinators.
*   [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=20) Difficulty Getting Started: It's hard to get things done when you *mean* to do them, *when* you mean to do them.
	* Starting a task is called [[task initiation]]
*   [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=30) [[Topics/Psychology/executive function/index|executive function]]: This is due to trouble with the brain's executive function system (goal setting and accomplishment).
*   [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=41) [[overwhelm]] and [[anxiety]]: Challenge knowing how/when to start, stay focused and finish leading to overwhelm and anxiety *before* starting.
*   [00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=53) Procrastination Cycle: Anxiety leads to procrastination to avoid discomfort.
	*   [01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=03) Vicious Cycle: Procrastination generates more anxiety, fueling further procrastination.

### Breaking the Cycle
*   [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=19) **Identify the Source**: Recognize that <u>the anxiety stems from *having* to do the task, not necessarily the task itself.</u>
*   [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=29) Anxiety Triggers: Anxiety is caused by not knowing how to start, how it will turn out, or how it's ever going to get done.
*   [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=37) Avoidance Amplifies Anxiety: Avoiding the task increases the anxiety.
*   [01:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=45) Completion Reduces Anxiety: Getting the task done eliminates the anxiety.
	* Remind yourself that you will feel less anxiety after completing the task

### Steps to Beat [[procrastination]]
####   [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=54) **One: Decide What to Do:**
- Identify the task. (A critical step)
####   [01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=59) **Two: Break it Down:** 
-  Break the task into small, manageable steps.
*   [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=00) Steps should be so small you think "Yeah, I can do that".
*   [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=05) This can be enough to reduce anxiety.
####   [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=08) **Three: Take the Next Step:** 
- Get specific and create cues:
*   [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=11) Examples: "If I wake up at six, I'll take a shower at seven." or "When Game of Thrones is over, I will outline my essay for 10 minutes."
####   [02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=21) **Four: Identify the Source of Anxiety:** Ask yourself what's causing your anxiety:
*   [02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=21) Am I bored?
*   [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=24) Am I afraid it won't be good enough?
*   [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=24) Am I afraid something bad will happen?
*   [02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=27) Am I stressed about something else?
####   [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=28) **Five: Correct the Issue:** Address the underlying cause of your anxiety.
*   [02:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=29) Stressed? Take a walk, breathe, meditate.
*   [02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=34) Focus on the present step, not the entire project.
*   [02:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=39) Bored? Find something interesting about the task, or play music while you work.
*   [02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=43) Challenge Negative Thoughts:
	*   [02:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=46) Don't automatically believe everything you think.
	*   [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=49) Example: Instead of "I'm never going to pass this class," try "Huh, I'm *having the thought* that I'm never going to pass this class. I wonder why?"
####   [02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=59) **Six: Get to Work:** Start even with anxiety, getting started helps reduce anxiety.
*   [03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=07) **Start Small**: Work for just five minutes, give yourself full permission to stop after.
*   [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=14) **Start Badly**: Type random letters or the worst first sentence you can think of.
*   [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=26) **Find the Right Time**: Work on procrastination-y tasks when you work best.
*   [03:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=34) **Find the Right Place**: Experiment with different environments to find what works best for you.
*   [03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=52) **Reward Yourself**: Associate something positive with the task to change your reaction.

### Last Minute Adrenaline Junkies
*   [04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=02) Anxiety as Motivation: Some people use procrastination anxiety to motivate themselves.
*   [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=10) Gradual Change: If anxiety is your cue to start, begin projects a little earlier each time.
*   [04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJx9sXeP34&t=33) Goal: Start on time and sleep the night before something is due.
