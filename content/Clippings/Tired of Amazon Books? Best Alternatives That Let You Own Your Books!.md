## Description
Are you tired of Amazon Kindle books and looking for a better way to actually own your ebooks📚? With Kindle books, you're often just licensing the content rather than truly owning it, meaning Amazon can remove or restrict access at any time. If you wan to know where to buy books online not Amazon, you're in the right place! I'll show you free, paid, and DRM-free Kindle alternatives that let you keep your ebooks. Whether you're searching for an online bookstore not Amazon, a more open ecosystem, or an e-reader that gives you full control, I've got you covered.
