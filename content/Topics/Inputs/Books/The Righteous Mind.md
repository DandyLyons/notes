## Takeaways 
**Jonathan Haidt's Six Moral Matrices**

- Jonathan Haidt's book "The Righteous Mind" introduces six moral matrices.
- These matrices are care/harm, liberty/oppression, fairness/cheating, loyalty/betrayal, authority/subversion, and sanctity/degradation.
- They explain how people evaluate morality differently, providing insight into political divides.
