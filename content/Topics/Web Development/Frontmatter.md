Frontmatter is a section commonly at the beginning of a [[Topics/Software Development/Web Development/Markdown/index|Markdown]] file[^1] that contains [[metadata]] about the file. It is typically written in [[YAML]] (though it can also be written in [[TOML]], [[JSON]], or other formats). 

The popular Markdown app Obsidian uses Frontmatter extensively to implement [[properties in Obsidian]].
