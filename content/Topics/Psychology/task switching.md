**Task switching**, or **set-shifting**, is an [[executive function]] that involves the ability to _unconsciously_ shift [[attention]] between one task and another. In contrast, [[cognitive shifting]] is a very similar executive function, but it involves _conscious_ (not unconscious) change in attention. Together, these two functions are subcategories of the broader [cognitive flexibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_flexibility "Cognitive flexibility") concept.
