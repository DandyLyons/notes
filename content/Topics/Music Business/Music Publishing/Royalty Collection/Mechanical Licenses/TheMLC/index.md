Issues blanket mechanical licenses for qualified streaming services  in the US such as Spotify, Apple Music etc. 

**Official website:** themlc.com 

> [!warning] Here be dragons!
> TheMLC is notorious for bad, incorrect data. Their entire job is to use data to distribute royalties to the right people, so if they have bad data, that is really news. Read more about it here: [Good data in Garbage Out: The MLC/HFA Data Disaster – The Trichordist](https://thetrichordist.com/2020/08/10/good-data-in-garbage-out-the-mlc-hfa-data-disaster/).
