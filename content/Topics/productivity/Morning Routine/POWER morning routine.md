## Routine
- [ ] **Word**: Start with reading the Bible to orient your heart toward God.
- [ ] **Prayer**: Spend time in prayer, worshipping God, confessing sin, thanking Him, and praying for the day ahead.
- [ ] **Exercise**: Engage in some physical activity, such as doing jumping jacks, to get the blood flowing and energize your body.
- [ ] **Reading and Writing**: Read a few pages from a book and write in your journal to clarify your thoughts and reflect on your spiritual journey.
- [ ] **Organization**: Look at your calendar, lay out your plan for the day, and identify your most important tasks to ensure a productive day ahead.
