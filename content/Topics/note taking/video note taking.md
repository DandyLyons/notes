## Workflows

### YouTube Note Taking
#### youtube-transcript.io
- https://www.youtube-transcript.io/
	- Paste in a YouTube URL
	- take notes

#### Time stamped note taking in Obsidian
- Prerequisites: 
	- Download [[Topics/Software/Obsidian/index|Obsidian]]
	- Download [[Media Notes]] plugin
- Usage: 
	- Add the `media_link` property to your Obsidian note
	- Put the YouTube URL in the `media_link` property
	- There should now be a YouTube video in your note. 
	- Optionally: Use the steps in [[#youtube-transcript.io]] to take advantage of a fully searchable, time-stamped transcript.
