### [PM devotional](https://ccel.org/meditate/01/09/pm) 
[[Luke-10#v42|Luke 10.42]] but **one thing is needed**. Mary has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her." 

Martha grumbled that Mary was not helping with the chores. This was because Martha was sitting at the feet of Jesus. She was intimately soaking in His teaching, and worshipping Him. Martha was missing the point. 
## Observations
Jesus taught her and us that **one thing is needed**, Christ. 

## Takeaways
I am so like Martha. I'm distracted be all of the *important things* but Jesus teaches me, that **only one thing is needed**, Christ.
