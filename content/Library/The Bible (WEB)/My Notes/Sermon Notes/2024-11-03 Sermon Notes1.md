## 📖Bible Passage
>[!NOTE]+ Isaiah 64:7
>There is no one who calls on your name, who stirs himself up to take hold of you; for you have hidden your face from us, and have consumed us by means of our iniquities. 

## Points

- We should learn two truths from [[Isa-64#v7|Isaiah 64:7]]: 
	- When we pray we must take hold of ourselves. 
		- We must pray yes, even in our prayers. Pray that we would stir ourselves up to take hold of our God. 
	- When we pray we must take hold of God. 
		- Plead with Him to show His face. 
		- To call upon His name. 
- Martin Luther said: "I have so much to do today. I better spend an extra hour in prayer."
- John Welsh: his wife said that he spent hours in prayer every day. He prayed family by family for all the 3000 people in his congregation
- We are tragically used to backsliding in our prayers. 
	- Yes, we are determined to change, to improve our prayer, but if we lean on ourselves we will fall back into our old ways. 
	- Our prayers are like toys that Satan can comfortably sleep next to, when they ought to be like missiles that attack the enemy of God.
	- Our prayers ought to be intimate, joyous times with our beloved Father which we delight in and look forward to. 
- How do we stir ourselves to take hold of God? 
	- William Carey: One of the first Christian missionaries to India, didn't convert a single person for 8 long years. 
		- He found it profitable to focus this time on prayer.
		- Sometimes we learn and grow more in our lives in seasons of waiting rather than in seasons of abundance. 
	- Thomas Watson: "It was the angel that fetched Peter out of the prison, but oh what joy for the Church, for it was prayer that fetched the angel out of Heaven."
- We need prayer warriors in the church. 
	- Allison: Sometimes older people are feeble and lament "I feel so useless". To them I say "Pray for the whole church. You could be the most valuable person in the church, simply by prayer."
	- Prayer is first if we really believe in God.
		- John Bunyan: "You can do more than prayer **after** you have prayed. But you can do no more than prayer **before** you have prayed." 
		- ❓: Why do we wait until we are a desperate situation before we pray? Pray beforehand. 
		- Pray before everything we do. 
			- Look through your busy list of things to do, and ask the Lord to help you to do each of those things. 
- Speak with **sincerity** in prayer. 
	- As soon as you bind yourself to a method of prayer, or a time minimum or any of these things, you are dooming yourself to cold selfish prayers. 
	- Banish every other thought. You are in God's presence now. Take hold of yourself. 
- Cultivate a continual spirit of prayer. 
	- [[1 Thess-05#v17|1 Thessalonians 5:17]]  What does it mean to **pray without ceasing**? 
		- It is a spirit of prayer. 
		- It does not mean that you are constantly muttering and speaking things to God. It means that you are regularly thinking about and in communion with God. 
			- When you get dressed in the morning pray "LORD, please clothe me in your righteousness this day."
- Pray intercedingly. 
	- We all pray selfishly. 
	- But we ought to pray for others, many others. 
		- It is difficult to hate or dislike others when you are praying for them. 
		- Your best friends are those who are praying for you. 
			- To intercede for someone is far more important than giving them an expensive gift. 
		- Ministers need your prayers. 
		- We all need prayers from those around us. 
		- Case study [[Num-12#v13|Numbers 12:13]]: Moses' sister Miriam jealously sought to usurp Moses' power and God smote her with leprosy. Did Moses revel in her pain? No he prayed and interceded on her behalf. 
- Use balance in prayer. Keep biblical balance in your prayers. 
	- [[Ps-11#v1|Psalm 11:1-3]] Adore God
	- Confess your sins to God like Daniel. 
- What does it mean to **take hold of God** in prayer? 
	- 1. It means to take hold of God's own promises found in the Bible.
		- Bring to God His own promises. 
		- It is no arrogancy to burden God with His own promises. 
		- "*Prayer is nothing but God's own promises reversed.*"
	- 2. Read the bible for prayer: 
		- Memorize it and meditate on it. 
		- Take every phrase and use it as a seed for your prayer. 
	- 3. Look to the glorious Trinity for prayer:
		- God prays to God. The Son prays to the Father and so forth. Follow Their example. 
		- The Holy Spirit stirs up prayer in us. 
		- The Son prays and intercedes for us on our behalf. 
		- And the Father has decreed our prayer. 
		- [[2 Cor-13#v14|2 Corinthians 13:14]] 
	- 4. Faith: You lay hold of God when you **really believe that God will answer your prayer.**: 
		- Yes we are unworthy of God answering our prayers, but He is a good Father. Why should we be surprised that He would fulfill His promises? 
		- This is why we can pray and keep on praying, even when we haven't gotten an answer yet, because we confidently know that God will provide an answer. 
		- Prayer is at the center of the Reformation. 
		- Climb up into the lap of God, and tell Him your needs, wishes, confessions.
