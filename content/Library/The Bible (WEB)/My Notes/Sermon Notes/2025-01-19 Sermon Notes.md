## Points
- "We are often like one-eyed people among half-blind people, not realizing it is possible to see God with both eyes." 
	- We know so little of our great God. 
	- If we knew more of Him, we would want to linger with Him and would not want to follow distractions. 
	- We may have been well instructed (in Doctrine) but we were poorly nourished (spiritually). 
	- Notice: We build massive cathedrals and church buildings, yet the least attended meeting is the prayer meeting. 
- We need a revival, but **a revival is not something that we can accomplish**. Only God can. 
	- What makes a revival a revival is not so much that God does that which He does not normally do. 
		- It is not like God does not ordinarily save and sanctify sinners. 
		- **What makes a revival a revival is that God does ordinary things extraordinarily.**
- Paul calls us to examine ourselves (v5). If we do so, then we would discover that we are not *disqualified*[^1] and this would truly be a wonderful discovery. 
- V6: Likewise Paul calls the congregation to examine Paul and his team. Likewise they would realize that Paul and his team were not disqualified. 
- ⭐: The knowledge of Christ leads to knowledge of self. You will not [[know thyself|know yourself]] until you know God. 
	- "*The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom.*" Proverbs 1:7
- **Disqualification of ministers**: ❓: What should we do when a minister is exposed to be disqualified because of their continual unrepentant sinful living? 
	- The sinful acts of a minister do not negate the truth of the Gospel which you heard. 
		- Allison: "There were people who heard the Gospel from Judas Iscariot and were converted."
- v8: We cannot do anything against the truth. 
	- To kick against the truth is to *kick against the goads*. 
	- [[2 Tim-03#v16|2 Tim 3.16]] Every Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness,  
	- [[John-17#v17|John 17.17]] Sanctify them in your truth. Your word is truth.  
	- ⭐: We have the truth from God. This cannot be taken away. 
		- But we must use, obey, read and believe in the truth. 
			- Worship the One who speaks the truth. 
			- What good is your Bible if it collects dust in your room? 
		- There are questions and struggles which confound us, **but the answers are found in this Book, the Bible**. 
- When God revives His church, He makes people <u>strong</u> only through an awareness of their <u>weakness</u>. 
	- Where is our strength? It's not in our birth, our wealth, our intellect, our accomplishments. 
		- All these things Paul called *manure*. These things control us and tangle us up in pride. 
	- Examples of humility and weakness: 
		- Isaiah said to God "Woe is me. I'm a man of unclean lips."
		- John admitted weakness on the island of Patmos when God gave him his vision. 
		- [[Gal-02#v20|Gal 2.20]] I have been crucified with Christ, and it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me. That life which I now live in the flesh, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself up for me.  
		- [[Ps-91#v2|Ps 91.2]] *I will say of Yahweh, "He is my refuge and my fortress; my God, in whom I trust."*  
			- **<u>God</u> is my refuge, not me.** 
- **With [[forgiveness]] comes [[holiness]].** We can't take one without the other: 
	- Holiness: 
		- Romans 12:1-2
		- 
- When God revives His church, God's people are brought up in apostolic teaching. 
	- v10: God gave authority to Paul. Paul is not a leader in the church because of his teaching or competencies or birth or anything in himself. He's a leader because God chose Paul <u>despite</u> his many weaknesses. 
	- The authority given to Paul is not a harsh authority, but an authority that loves, builds, teaches and applies the Gospel. 
	- These are the marks of revival. When God revives His church, they value the sound teaching of the Word of God. 
		- God's people are no longer weak scattered sheep, but they are His army. 
			- ❓Are you a part of God's army, who follows the Lamb? Come to Jesus today and put your trust in Him? He saves to the uttermost.
