## 📖Bible Passage
>[!NOTE]+ [[2 Cor-13#v14|2 Corinthians 13.14]] 
>The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, God's love, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen. 


## Points

- Today we consider our **response** to the love of God
- God calls us to cultivate **genuine** gratitude for the love that God shows to us. 
- How should we respond in appreciation? 
	- How we respond to someone's love really does matter. **Unrequited love** is painful and causes loneliness. 
- God shows his love in selfless sacrifice, compassionate generosity, undeserved mercy and grace. 
- If you are a child of God, then the God of Heaven loves you. 
- Many people misuse the doctrine of God's love and abuse His love. 
	- They say things like since **God is love** that means He will love me no matter what. 
		- It is true that God is good to all beings. 
			- The fact that God does not immediately strike us dead for our sins shows God's incredible patience and kindness towards all. 
	- Many people fail to see the entirety of what is taught in [[John-03#v16|John 3.16]]. 
		- >For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.  
			- 1. God loved the world
			- 2. He gave His son
			- 3. Whoever believes in Him will have eternal life. 
		- Many people only look at and focus on the first part, that God loves us, but neglect the next two parts. 
	- ⭐ When you reject the Gospel you are rejecting the love of God. 
	- ⭐ When you reject the love of God, you are rejecting the very best love that has or ever will be. 
		- It is **when trouble comes** that we see whether love is truly genuine. 
			- It takes great faith to love someone even in bad times. It takes great faith to love God even when affliction strikes us. 
			- When we are troubled we feel compelled to ask *Does God still love me?*
				- This is the wrong question. We should ask *What is God teaching me through this trouble?* 
				- [[Heb-12#v11|Hebrews 12.11]] All chastening seems for the present to be not joyous but grievous; yet afterward it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been trained by it.  
- Case Study: John Wesley was impressed by the Moravians when they lived out joy and gladness, despite being tossed by a terrifying storm. How? It was because they were **sure of God's love**. 

## Application
- How else should we respond to God's love? **Be assured of God's love toward you.**
	- When we are distraught, we need encouragement and comfort. We need to be reminded of the truth of God's love toward us. 
	- [[Luke-11#v13|Luke 11.13]] If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give The Holy Spirit to those who ask him?"  
		- Remember that God, your Father loves you! Cling to this and do not forget it. This truth will sustain you through great troubles. 
	- The enemy tries to deceive us into believing that God does not love us. 
		- This is what the serpent did to Eve. He caused Eve to question God's love toward her. He implied that God was trying to withhold love from her. 
- You must care about the souls of other people. You must communicate to your neighbors, the love that God has toward them, and their desperate need for it. 
	- Show them your need for it. How God loved you even when you did not deserve it. And implore them *If God can love a wretched sinner like me, then surely He loves you.* 
	- No man can convert a sinner. The Holy Spirit must work in their heart. But what does the Holy Spirit use to convert sinners? Faithful Christians. 
		- You can and must tell others of the love of Christ. 
- Finally, we show appreciation for God's love by **showing love toward each other**. Christians should love each other. 
	- [[1 John-04#v11|1 John 4.11]] Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.  
	- [[1 John-03#v16|1 John 3.16]] By this we know love, because he laid down his life for us. And we ought to lay down our lives for the brothers.  
	- By *ought*, The Holy Spirit means this is your **debt**. We owe a response to God's love. 
		- We owe our entire lives to God. We owe our salvation to God. We owe **everything** to God. 
	- What if it is difficult to love another. 
		- 1. Get your facts straight. When you do not love another believer it is usually because of some misunderstanding. 
		- 2. **Get to know each other**. 
			- [[Mal-03#v16|Malachi 3.16]] Then those who feared Yahweh spoke one with another; and Yahweh listened, and heard, and a book of memory was written before him, for those who feared Yahweh, and who honored his name.  
		- 3. **Forgive your brother or sister**. How can you not forgive your brother, when they haven't hurt you nearly as much as you hurt Christ, who forgave you
		- 4. **Pray for one another**. 
		- 5. **Mortify those things that inhibit love**. 
			- [[1 Pet-02#v1|1 Peter 2.1]] Putting away therefore all wickedness, all deceit, hypocrisies, envies, and all evil speaking,  
		- 6. **Drink deeply of the love of God and Christ**. 
			- This is the best cure for not loving? 
			- When is the last time that you took a fresh sip of the love of God? 
				- When did you last pause and consider the sheer magnificence of the love of God. 
				- Consider the costliness and intensity of God's love for you. 
				- Pray that God will transcribe some of His love toward you, so that you might love those around you.
