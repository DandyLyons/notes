## Points

- ❓: where will you turn for hope, joy, and satisfaction in 2025? 
	- Let’s make it a year of growing in the grace and knowledge of Christ. 
- Come unto God for true satisfaction by **hearing His word**. 
	- God invites to come to Him and enjoy a feast
	- He invites the poor and hungry
	- His feast is free, unlike the pleasures of this world. 
		- Eating at the table of idols is **dissatisfying** : [[Isa-44#v20|Isa 44.20]]
	- His feast is **satisfying** unlike this world
		- [[John-04#v13|John 4:13-14]][[John-04#v14|]] 
	- To come unto God is to hear His Word
		- You are **invited** and **commanded** to listen 
		- God promised an everlasting kingdom to David’s son (Jesus). 
- Seek the Lord now as God calls you to repent
	- We do this by forsaking sin and returning to the Lord **daily**. 
	- [[Ps-130#v3|Ps 130:3-8]][[Ps-130#v4|]][[Ps-130#v5|]][[Ps-130#v6|]][[Ps-130#v7|]][[Ps-130#v8|]] If God kept a record of sins, then who could stand? But there is forgiveness with God. 
	- [[Ps-103#v11|Ps 103:11-12]][[Ps-103#v12|]] 
	- [[Heb-09#v26|Heb 9.26]]: Jesus sacrificed Himself once for all. 
		- He does not need to resacrifice Himself every time we sin. 
	- >His grace and mercy are beyond anything we could imagine. **But don’t presume on His grace** - Spurgeon
	- >The grace that does not change my life, will not save my soul. - Spurgeon 
	- We should always be aware of the greatness of God. Respect Him. Do not speak flippantly about Him. 
- The LORD provides **true joy** through His powerful Word:
	- [[James-01#v18|James 1.18]] Of his own will he gave birth to us by the **word of truth**, that we should be a kind of first fruits of his creatures.  
	- [[1 Pet-01#v23|1 Pet 1.23]] having been born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, through the **word of God**, which lives and remains forever.  
	- **Our mission is to simply proclaim God’s Word.** But it is God who works through the Word and saves sinners. Not us. 
	- Isaiah 55 depicts a renewed world, with a lifted curse, rejoicing God. 
		- This points to the Jews returning from exile to the promise land. 
		- And it points forward to Christ’s second coming. 

## Application
- Have you come unto the Lord for salvation and hope? 
- Do you daily continue to come unto Jesus feeding on His Word? 
- Are you seeking God daily in prayer? 
	- The Christian life is daily seeking Him. 
	- It’s not perfection. It’s direction. 
- Is your greatest joy knowing God in His Word?
