- [[James-04#v7|James 4.7]] Be subject therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

	- [[Luke-04#v8|Luke 4.8]] Jesus answered him, "Get behind me Satan! For it is written, 'You shall worship the Lord your God, and you shall serve him only.'"

	- [[Luke-04#v13|Luke 4.13]] When the devil had completed every temptation, he departed from him until another time.
