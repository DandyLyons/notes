The Greek word apologia (ἀπολογία) means "==speaking in defense==" and refers to a formal defense of a position, opinion, or action.


> [!NOTE] [[1 Pet-03#v15|1 Pet 3.15]]
>  But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts. Always be ready to give an **<u>answer</u>** (ἀπολογία) to everyone who asks you a reason concerning the hope that is in you, with humility and fear,
