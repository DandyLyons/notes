## Description
I chat with Jonas Tyroller ( @JonasTyroller ) about how he sold over 960,000 copies of his game. ► Play Thronefall: https://store.steampowered.com/app/223915...

## My Notes


### How the [[Topics/Design/index|Design]] Process Works
- [04:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=265&v=hglxTerNK2U): How [[Topics/Design/index|Design]] Works: 
	- 1. Define design **goals** and **constraints** 
	- 2. [05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=302&v=hglxTerNK2U) Start a search process
		- Make the assumption that you cannot guess the best solution. 
		- Experiment with different approaches and gain experience. 
			- Don't fall for the myth that you will find the best approach on the first try. 
	- 3. "Speed mode"
		- Less experimentation. Focus on iteration and improvement. 
	- My thoughts: Jonas's design process reminds me a lot of [[The Laws of Creativity]]. 

### The Role of [[Taste]]
- [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=724&v=hglxTerNK2U): Thomas: I notice this design process depends on **taste**, the ability to discern what is *good*. 
	- Jonas: I describe taste as **measurement**
- [13:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=787&v=hglxTerNK2U): Common mistake: making only one prototype
	- Devs make a prototype, show it to a friend, and then they make **that** game. 
	- This is pointless, because you don't have anything to compare your prototype to. 
	- [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=838&v=hglxTerNK2U): You need objective measurement, not just relative measurements

### Why Prototype? 
- [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=860&v=hglxTerNK2U): Q: What is the point of [[Topics/Design/Prototyping/index|Prototyping]]? 
	- "It is easier to **choose** the best idea than it is to **guess** the best idea." 

### Early Access
- [17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1058&v=hglxTerNK2U): Start with the abstract, prove the abstract, and then you refine it
- [19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1146&v=hglxTerNK2U): Q: Would you consider Early Access to be the part of the process to refine, or should it already be refined? 
	- [20:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1204&v=hglxTerNK2U): Early Access is essentially already your **Main Launch**. 
		- You only get one launch. 
		- If early access doesn't go well, then your second launch isn't gonna go well either. 
		- [20:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1258&v=hglxTerNK2U): Thronefall strategy: The game was already refined but it had only 3 levels. Very little content. And added much more content over the next 18 months. 
			- The second release was not as successful as the first one. 
			- [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1292&v=hglxTerNK2U): We look at early access as a **scaling device**. 
	- [22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1377&v=hglxTerNK2U): In Early Access you can get away with a <u>small game</u>, but you cannot get away with a <u>bad game</u>. 
		- You can scale it up after. 
- [23:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1424&v=hglxTerNK2U): Q: What kind of games are good for early access? 
	- Games that you can imagine working on for a long time. 
	- [23:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1437&v=hglxTerNK2U): "Early Access communicates to the player that we're still actively working on this game."
	- [24:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1455&v=hglxTerNK2U): Early Access only really works with **replayable** games. 
- [24:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1479&v=hglxTerNK2U): Early Access is not [[Topics/Design/Prototyping/index|Prototyping]]. 
- [24:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1491&v=hglxTerNK2U): How much do you listen to your audience as you get feedback from Early Access? 
	- [25:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1510&v=hglxTerNK2U): A: "We should have listened to players more when we made Thronefall". 

### Feedback
- [28:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1690&v=hglxTerNK2U): Q: Who should listen to and who should we not listen to? 
	- [29:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1762&v=hglxTerNK2U): A: The first people we should listen to are **other successful game developers**. 
		- Players don't always know what they like. 
	- [30:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1814&v=hglxTerNK2U): A: Second, listen to the players. 
		- **The tricky thing about listening to players is that you need to read between the lines.** 
		- ⭐: [30:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=1840&v=hglxTerNK2U): Listen to the **problems** that players have rather than the solutions that players have.
